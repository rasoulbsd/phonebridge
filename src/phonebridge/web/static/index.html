            This connection

            Mac (full iMessage)

            A Mac signed in to your Apple ID and running BlueBubbles Server brings group chats, photos and files, reactions, replies, typing indicators and FaceTime links.

            iPhone over Bluetooth

            • Your PC talks to the iPhone directly, with no cloud account in between. History starts when this app first connected and is saved on this PC.
            • Some things aren't available over Bluetooth. Group chats, photos and other attachments, reactions, and messages you send from the iPhone itself don't come through. This is an iPhone limitation.
            • Keep your iPhone within Bluetooth range with Bluetooth on. If it wanders off, the app reconnects by itself when it's back.
            Microsoft Phone Link is using the connection.
